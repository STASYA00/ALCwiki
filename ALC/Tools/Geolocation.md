Is your problem linked to #geo location? Need to find the location of a particular place or extract a set of satellite images?

Apart from several open [[🌏 Maps|Maps]] datasets you can check out a set of existing tools used for geolocation and working with images on the maps!

*Use them in your projects or take them as a base or reference for your own tools!*
### Tools & References

* [Size of the objects from images](https://www.bellingcat.com/resources/2023/09/07/measuring-up-how-to-calculate-the-size-of-objects-in-open-source-material/) by [Bellingcat](https://www.bellingcat.com/)
* [OpenStreetMap Search Tool](https://osm-search.bellingcat.com/) by [Bellingcat](https://www.bellingcat.com/), [how it works](https://www.bellingcat.com/resources/how-tos/2023/05/08/finding-geolocation-leads-with-bellingcats-openstreetmap-search-tool/) and the [source code](https://github.com/bellingcat/osm-search)
* [OSMFinder](https://github.com/Xetnus/osm-finder) - semi-automated geolocation of images by osm features
* [Cloud-free Sentinell imagery](https://github.com/bellingcat/cloud-free-subregion) by [Bellingcat](https://www.bellingcat.com/)
* [Geolocating images with Deep Learning](https://geospy.ai/) with a ~free tier [API](https://api.geospy.ai/); okay precision
* [Time-lapsed Satellite imagery](https://apps.sentinel-hub.com/) and how to [work with it](https://www.bellingcat.com/resources/how-tos/2018/11/19/using-time-lapse-satellite-imagery-detect-infrastructure-changes-case-studies-via-myanmar-nigeria-south-china-sea/) by [Bellingcat](https://www.bellingcat.com/)
* [ShadowFinder](https://github.com/bellingcat/ShadowFinder)


_Note: some of these projects are non-commercial or relatively new. It means they can have bugs, be unmaintained or non user-friendly. They can still be taken as a reference and a learning from the creator's experience._
