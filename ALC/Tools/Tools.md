Here are some tool packages that can be used to develop your own projects or to take inspiration from! You can even contribute to their development by adding new features ✨

#### Collections for AEC

🌏   [[Geolocation]] tools - solutions related to locating images or text on a map
🏡   [[Spatial]] tools - everything related to spatial analysis
🔋   [[Performance]] tools - ~~almost~~ ready solutions for evaluating building performance
🔛   [[Interplatform]] tools - off-the-shelf products to work on the same model in different programs at the same time 💜

#### General Tool Collections

❔  [Bellingcat Toolkit](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit) - collection of tools for OSINT, [Geo](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit/categories/geolocation) and [Images](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit/categories/image-video) might be the most relevant
