Working with apartment layout or a district map and in need of some tools to start the analysis with?

Check out the open [[🌏 Maps|Maps]] and [[🏘 Interior layout]] datasets aggregated by us or take a look at this set of existing tools.

*Use them in your projects or take them as a base or reference for your own tools!*
### Tools & References

* [Space Syntax](https://www.spacesyntax.online/software-and-manuals/) - basic analysis of a building layout
* [Matterport](https://matterport.com/) - 3D space scan with a phone camera


_Note: some of these projects are non-commercial or relatively new. It means they can have bugs, be unmaintained or non user-friendly. They can still be taken as a reference and a learning from the creator's experience._
